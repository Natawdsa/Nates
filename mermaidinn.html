Six Centuries of Story

Built from the ruins of fire

When French Raiders burned Rye to the ground on June 22nd, 1377, they could not have known they were writing the opening chapter of England's most remarkable inn. The Mermaid rose from those ashes, rebuilt in 1420 with Caen stone fireplaces, wattle and daub walls, and secret staircases that have hidden priests, smugglers, and royalty alike.

Queen Elizabeth I passed through these very rooms in 1573 on her royal procession. Two centuries later, the notorious Hawkhurst Gang — six hundred strong — claimed the Giant's Fireplace Bar as their smugglers' headquarters. The cellars beneath your feet were first laid in 1156.
