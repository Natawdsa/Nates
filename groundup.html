Welcome — Ground Up, Rye

"Built from the ground up — by one person, with one idea: that Rye deserved better coffee and a proper place to think."

Esther opened Ground Up because she believed a town as alive as Rye deserved a coffee shop to match. Not a chain. Not a compromise. Something real.

Every cup is made with local Rye Bay Coffee Beans, pulled to order. The food comes from the award-winning Sussex Kitchen. The teas are sourced properly. Nothing here is an afterthought.

Founded by Esther

Ground Up isn't just a coffee shop — it's a room where things happen. Where the early risers plan their next move, where strangers become regulars, where the chess board is always set up.

Whether you're here for twenty minutes or the whole afternoon, you're welcome. Bring your dog. Bring your laptop. Just leave your bad mood at the door.
